Feelings Untold

Love

At the very start, we were on the same page...

At the very start,
We were on the same page,
I could read your mind,
And you heard what's on my heart...

But as the road went long,
Something begun to change,
I lost sight of the gem,
That I had kept hidden from you...

Hoping one day I'll show you,
In exchange for happiness,
And a heart of resonance,
Too bad that day wasn't meant to be...

Looking at your tired eyes,
Our road seemed uninteresting as it went on,
Losing track of what to say,
Became a habit I can't explain...

Until you made a hurting choice,
To change lanes,
Leaving me here,
Talking to the whispering wind.

Closed Chapter

Love

I guess this is how it is for us...

I guess this is how it is for us,
Two distant hearts,
Sharing the same pulse,
Sitting on untold thoughts...

Afraid of the pain we'd bring,
Upon our already burdened souls,
Minds torn apart,
By the truth we can't escape...

We smile to hide the pain,
Screaming out I need you,
In the depth of loneliness,
Wondering what lives we both live,
Without each other around.

Just Friends

Love

They told each other...

They told each other,
"Till the end of the line"
Only death, they said,
Could ever pull them apart.

Deep down, the truth was known,
But the feelings stayed unspoken.
As time passed,
The cracks began to show.

Both longed to be free,
Yet the bond was too strong to sever.
A love so rare,
Their hearts burned quietly in its flame.

But as the saying goes:
Never awaken love before it's time.
This one never saw the light of day,
Yet it lived,
Somewhere in the realm of the unseen.

Moved On

Love

I heard your voice...

I heard your voice,
But it held a tone,
So different,
From the one I once knew.

I smiled,
Guided by what my eyes trusted.

But as you spoke,
I saw it clearly,
The person I loved,
Lived only in memory,
Lost somewhere in the past.

A tear traced my cheek,
As reality whispered,
"It's time to let go."

My Dear

Love

I've watched you...

I've watched you for a while,
Seen your tears fall in the dark,
Holding pain in quiet hands,
While telling the world you're fine.

You are a treasure,
Too rare to measure,
Refined beneath the weight of pressure,
With a heart still soft.. still kind.

I wonder how many scars you've worn,
Just to paint that smile,
How many lies you had to whisper,
Just to feel peace for a while.

I almost fell in love with you,
But I came to understand,
We weren't meant to stay,
Only to meet..
And help each other find the way.

No More Tears Left to Cry

Melancholy

I sat by the riverside...

I sat by the riverside,
Watching stars ripple,
In the still water's glow,
As the moon smiled quietly above.

I sighed....
A soft breath of release,
Closing my eyes,
Just for a moment,

To imagine what could be,
What I might lose,
And the knives I've held,
Pulled from the backs
Of those who once offered love.

In that silence,
My heart sank,
Realizing how distant I've drifted,
From my Creator,
Yet sensing His nearness.

It's a war,
I seem destined to fight,
Grace beside guilt,
Faith beside fear.

I opened my eyes,
One tear escaped,
Not from pain,
But from knowing,

This world is vanity,
And still,
I pray I keep
My sanity.

Where is the Lie?

Inner Battles

You must think...

You must think,
I speak only to be seen,
mistaking confession for vanity,
noise for need.

You skim the surface
And call it depth,
So I press the blade closer,
not to wound,
But to jolt the pulse back into feeling.

I paint myself onto this vacant space,
Hoping God leans in somewhere between the strokes,
that you might sense what is not said,
two tides held behind calm water,
oceans restrained,
exchanged as shallow pools of speech.

Cracked Shell

Inner Battles

What if I told you the truth...

What if I told you the truth,
About the man I truly am,
The scars I carefully hide,
The smile I trained myself to wear,
The heart I shattered to keep standing.

The words I've spoken behind backs,
In rooms of strangers I called "close,"
What a bitter reflection it is,
When I'm left alone with the mirror,
Haunted by the masks I've worn.

So I whisper to the world,
And cry to my Creator in the same breath,
A fragile prayer, stitched with trembling faith,
Hoping He still listens to broken tongues,
Hoping His mercy can heal what my hands destroy.

Tired of building castle walls of steel,
Holding the cure to the poison I still drink,
I find myself driven to a corner—
Talking to myself for normalcy,
Reminding my soul to hold on,
Before vanity takes it all away.

The Eyes Never Lie

Inner Battles

It was always there...

It was always there,
Written in silence,
Etched in glances
I refused to understand.

I wore the blindfold willingly,
Calling it trust,
Calling it hope,
While truth stared back at me,
Unblinking.

Words reached my ears
But never touched my soul.
I filtered out the warnings,
Held onto illusions,
Like they were lifelines.

Now the veil is gone,
Not torn by me,
But by time,
Or a stranger's whisper.

And though I ask, "Is it too late?",
I know time bends,
And healing doesn't check the clock.

Maybe I'll find the moments I lost,
Catch the light I once ignored,
And see the world again,
Not as I wished it to be,
But as it truly is.

Good Riddance

Melancholy

Look at us now. What was all that supposed to be?

Look at us now. What was all that supposed to be? Friends… wasn't that the point from the very beginning? Strange how I sit here writing about something that only exists because of the choices I made. I tell myself it's no longer my burden, yet the thought still circles back.

Sometimes I wonder if there was meaning in any of it. Was it a lesson I needed, or just another mistake people later rename as growth? Maybe it was inevitable. Maybe we were never meant to stay in the same story. Not everything broken was meant to be fixed.

So I hope you're alright. I hope life treated you kindly. I hope you found people who see you the way I once tried to. As for me, I'll leave it here— not with anger, not even with regret, just the quiet understanding that some chapters close without asking permission.

Good riddance… and goodbye.

Glass Wall

Melancholy

Everyone is watching— waiting to see what you'll pull this time.

Everyone is watching— waiting to see what you'll pull this time. I hope you know what you're doing. I hope the voice in your head is strong enough, and right enough, to guide you. Yes, you've changed. That much is certain. Change is natural. But at what cost?

You feel it too—every time you close your eyes, asking God why you choose to wound yourself when you could heal, let go, and be like the ones you admire— the smiling faces posted for the world, the voices telling you to change before you reach a destination they've already judged. It lingers, that unease you can't quite explain.

So I wonder—will the day really come when you live the picture you paint so clearly? Or will it remain pressed behind glass, visible, untouchable— a life you study from the outside while everyone keeps watching?

Cracked Reflections

Melancholy

You called me selfish. The word cut deeper than expected.

You called me selfish. The word cut deeper than expected. Aren't we all, in quieter ways? That's what happens when you confuse kindness with the need to be needed.

I avoided that truth for years. Look at these lines— they orbit the poet again. A narrative shaped around my own pulse, dragging the reader along. I wish there were another method. But this is the incision point.

If there is a final act, it won't be dramatic. Just a quiet smile toward the sky, back pressed to the earth, birds indifferent, sun unbothered.

Maybe then you'll understand— the knives were never for spectacle. I let them cut so the pain would be visible.

Perhaps that's all this ever was: not a grand confession, just proof that something inside was wounded.

Until When?

Inner Battles

You think you understand it because the title feels obvious.

You think you understand it because the title feels obvious. That is the first misstep— naming something and believing it's known.

You hold your ideals close, call them conviction, call yourself right.

Let's turn the lens. Step into another circle— one you recognize, even if you'd rather not. It isn't polished. It isn't flattering. But it is there.

I watch you draw lines only to step across them. Set boundaries only to soften them. Heal— just enough to be wounded again.

And I ask quietly— is this devotion, or drift? Are you living for Christ, or merely surviving under His name?

Strange, isn't it— to cling so fiercely to life, while knowing it must be surrendered in the end.

So tell me— until when?
